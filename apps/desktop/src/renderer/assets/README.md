## Banner de empresas

`companies-banner.webp` é a fotografia gerada com a ferramenta integrada da OpenAI para a proposta aprovada “Carteira em cartões”. Também é empacotada localmente.

Prompt usado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic photographic banner for the Companies management screen of ContabiliNico, a Brazilian accounting desktop application. Create a refined candid editorial photo of an accountant meeting a small-business owner in a contemporary Brazilian accounting office. An adult Brazilian woman accountant with dark shoulder-length hair, elegant navy business casual blouse, and an adult Brazilian man business owner in a light blue shirt, sitting together at an oak meeting table and discussing an organized folder of documents and a laptop. Focused friendly natural expressions, neither looking at camera, realistic hands and anatomy. Soft daylight, tasteful pale neutral decor, glass partition and understated plant, warm professional mood. Wide panoramic composition around 3:1. Place people and meeting in the RIGHT TWO THIRDS; the LEFT THIRD is a plain deep navy office wall out of focus with generous low-detail negative space for a UI text overlay. Keep generous room above their heads. Palette matches navy #111c2e, indigo #455fdb, cool pale gray #f5f7fb, oak accents. Premium corporate editorial photography, authentic and calm. No text, no letters, no readable documents or screens, no logos, no currency symbols, no watermarks, no UI elements, no headline in the image.

## Banner da visão geral

`office-team-banner.webp` é uma imagem gerada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens da OpenAI para o protótipo aprovado “Análise do período”. O arquivo é empacotado localmente; a tela não faz pedidos de imagens pela rede.

Prompt usado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic photographic banner for the overview of ContabiliNico, a Brazilian accounting desktop application. Create a premium, natural editorial photograph of a small accounting team working together: two Brazilian adult accountants in their thirties, a brown-skinned woman with shoulder-length dark hair and a Black man with short hair, candid and relaxed, focused on reviewing printed financial documents together at a tidy modern desk with an open laptop. Authentic business casual clothing in light blue, cream and navy, believable proportions and hands, no staged thumbs-up or looking at camera. Bright contemporary accounting office with soft daylight from a window, oak details and understated plants; professional, warm and calm. Wide panoramic composition about 3:1. Keep both people and the desk in the RIGHT TWO THIRDS with generous space around their heads. The LEFT THIRD is a plain deep navy office wall softly out of focus, low detail and darker lighting, clean negative space reserved for UI headline overlay. Palette harmonizes with navy #111c2e, indigo #455fdb and pale gray #f5f7fb. Photographic realism, subtle natural film texture, refined restrained corporate editorial art direction. No text, no logos, no letters, no readable screens or documents, no watermarks, no infographic elements, no UI mockup. This image itself contains no headline; UI text will be added separately.
